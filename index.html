The problem

You already know you are making the same mistakes. You just cannot see them while they are happening.

Post-game analysis tells you what went wrong after the result is decided. By then the position is cold, the tension is gone, and the lesson does not attach to anything. The mistake repeats next game.

Live coaching

He interrupts before the blunder, not after.

When the position turns critical, GM Forge stops the game. His arm extends across the board and his finger lands on the exact square that matters. Then he asks — and the board stays locked until you answer.

Every move he names comes straight out of the engine's own lines for that exact position. He cannot invent a move, and he stays quiet when nothing is happening.

Training that rewires

Your mistakes become the curriculum.

Every blunder you make is classified, stored, and turned into a drill built from your own games. Then the drill comes back on a schedule designed around how memory actually works.

Spaced repetition

Patterns return at widening intervals, timed to arrive just before you would forget. It feels harder than re-reading. That difficulty is the mechanism.

Confidence-rated correction

We ask how sure you are before showing the answer. Mistakes you were confident about correct hardest and stick longest.

Retrieval, not recognition

You play the move on a real board rather than picking it from a list. Recognising an answer is not the same as finding it.

Interleaving

Once a pattern is half-learned we mix it with others, so you first have to work out which problem you are facing — the real over-the-board skill.

Built on the forgetting curve (Ebbinghaus), desirable difficulties (Bjork), learning from errors (Metcalfe), and retrieval practice (Karpicke & Roediger).

Adaptive opponent

The bot meets you where you actually are.

Your first game calibrates. From then on the engine plays at a level tuned to how you really play, not to a number you typed in — strong enough to punish the mistakes you make, not so strong that nothing is learnable.
