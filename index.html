The problem

You keep losing to the same mistakes — and nobody ever told you which ones.

Every chess site hands a 900 and a 1900 the exact same puzzles. Generic lessons. Generic openings. A rating graph that goes up and down and never explains why.

So you plateau. You hang the same bishop. You rush the same queen out. You lose the same won endgames — over and over — with no idea it's a pattern.

How it works

Three steps. Then a coach who actually knows you.

  1. 01

    Play one game on your own

    No coach, no hints. Stockfish reads every move and works out where — and how badly — it went wrong. One of these a day is what keeps your rating and your profile honest, and it unlocks coached play for the rest of the day.

  2. 02

    Get your Thinking Profile

    Not just what you got wrong — why. Eight habits, scored out of 100: Idea Range, Threat Radar, Impulse Control, Search Width, Calculation Depth, Risk Read, Board Awareness, Composure. Read only from games you play without the coach, because those are the honest ones.

  3. 03

    Play with your live coach

    Now play. GM Forge sits beside you, points at the exact square, and speaks on every move. Stuck? "I don't know what to do here" walks you through it — count the attackers and defenders, decide what is really loose, then choose. He never just hands you the move.

Puzzles from your own blunders

Every mistake becomes a position to solve, and they rotate so you never get the same five twice. Run out and it builds fresh ones that need the same thinking — not a repeat of something you have already seen.

Training built from your real games

Weighted nine to one towards games you played without the coach, so it drills what you get wrong alone. Widening intervals — 1, 3, 7, 16, 35 days — with a streak that pays more XP the longer it runs.

Watch both moves play out

Torn between two moves? Draw an arrow on each and he plays both forward on the board, a move at a time, then asks which you would rather have. Say you are not sure and he explains before he ever reveals.

Questions

The short answers.

What is ChessForge?

A chess-improvement tool built around a live coach. GM Forge sits beside you while you play a calibrated bot, points at the squares that matter and stops you before you go wrong — then turns the mistakes you do make into drills that come back until the pattern stops happening.

Do I need to be good at chess?

No. ChessForge meets you where you are — the bot calibrates to your level and the coaching is tuned to your weaknesses, whether you're 600 or 1900. Beginners get the most out of it, honestly.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — one click through the Stripe billing portal, no emails, no hoops. You keep Grandmaster until the end of the period you already paid for.

How is this different from Chess.com analysis?

Chess.com tells you a move was a blunder — after the game. ChessForge finds the pattern behind your blunders, names your thinking style, builds drills and puzzles from your own losses, and then coaches you during the game so the mistake never happens. Personalised, not generic.
