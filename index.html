Questions

The ones people
actually ask.

How is this different from an engine?

An engine tells you the move and the number. GM Forge tells you what your move runs into, on which square, and what it costs in material — while you can still take it back. Every move he names comes from Stockfish; he is not allowed to invent one.

Why does the free plan make me play one game on my own?

Because that game is the measurement. Your play strength, your accuracy and every drill you get are read from unaided games. Coaching before that would be built on nothing.

Does it get better the more I play?

Yes, and specifically: the drills change. Patterns your games keep producing get served more often, and ones you have stopped falling for get spaced further apart.

What rating range is this for?

Roughly 300 to 1000. It is built for the point where you lose to the same three or four things over and over and cannot see which ones they are.

Do I have to give you my chess.com password?

No. The import reads the games chess.com already publishes on its public API. Your username is all it needs.

Does it work on my phone?

Dashboard, Training, Puzzles and Shop do. Play & Coach and Analysis need a bigger screen — they put a board, a graded move list and the coach side by side, and stacked on a phone those stop being one thing you can read.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, from your account menu, including during the trial.
